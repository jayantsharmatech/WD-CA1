Trainee Success Stories

These are real journeys of young people who turned their first chance into a new beginning at The Step-Up Cafe. [file:1]

Priya – From Rejection to Confidence

Priya faced constant rejection due to a lack of experience and felt anxious about her future. [file:1] At the cafe, she completed a 3‑month training in barista skills, teamwork, and customer communication and is now a full‑time shift supervisor.

Rahul – First Job, First Step

Rahul dropped out of school and struggled to find stable work. With hands-on training at the cafe, he learned punctuality, POS billing, and food safety and later joined a large coffee chain.

Ayesha – Supporting Her Family

Ayesha wanted to support her younger siblings but lacked guidance. The cafe gave her mentoring, soft skills workshops, and part-time work, helping her fund her college education.
